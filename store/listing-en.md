## Full description (max. 4000)
Welcome to Matijuego, the Matichoc arcade! 🍫

Pick your Matichico, change their hair, clothes, hats and much more in the shop with the Matipoints you earn by playing, and explore 14 games and 3D worlds, plus a workshop to create your own chocolate, all made with lots of cacao:

🧗 CHOCO PARKOUR — An endless world! Run and jump across platforms that move, break or blink through 10 worlds (meadows, caramel, clouds, galaxies…), grab power-ups 🧲🛡️⚡🦘 and beat your distance record. There is also a 7-stage adventure (with a house to climb, walls, red lines and a tricky stage) with a cape, goggles and a crown.

🏘️ MATICHOC TOWN — Stroll through a 3D town with a chocolate fountain, boutique, factory and an arcade with magic doors. Say hi to the neighbors, collect golden alfajores and watch night fall.

🏠 MY HOUSE AND PETS — Decorate your own room with rocket beds, arcade machines and mini chocolate fountains, and adopt a pet that follows you in every game.

🎃🎄 SEASONAL EVENTS — Halloween, Christmas, Chile's national holidays and more: the town transforms and you can win exclusive items by playing.

⭐ MY ADVENTURE — Level up, complete 3 new missions every day, keep your streak and fill your chocolate album with shiny stickers.

🏃 COLLECT AND RUN — Race through 3D courts and gyms, jump hurdles and grab treats before time runs out.

🧱 PRODUCT BLOCKS — Fit shiny chocolate cubes into your display case and complete lines.

🏀 BASKETBALL SHOTS — Set your aim and power, score on the golden hoop and hear the crowd cheer.

🤸 CHEERLEADER JUMPS — Tap to the beat with your team: tosses, mid-air tricks and the final pyramid.

🦘 CHOCO JUMP · 🏎️ CHOCOLATE CARS · 🧠 MATICHOC MEMORY — More adventures: climb non-stop, dodge on the road and find matching pairs of treats.

🍬 MATI MERGE — Merge identical alfajores, cuchuflís, bonbons and chocolate bars to reach the Mati Dubai, and deliver your customers' orders.

⚽ CHOCO SOCCER · 🍫 CHOCO MATCH · 🍄 CHOCO ADVENTURE · 🏙️ CHOCO CITY — Four games to unlock with the Matipoints you earn by playing! Penalties, curling free kicks and goalkeeping; a match-3 with Matichoc products and bosses; a platform adventure across 5 worlds; and an open city to drive around and help the neighbors with relaxed missions.

🍫 CREATE YOUR CHOCOLATE — Choose the product, flavor, toppings and wrapper, and design your own label. A new challenge with Matipoints every day!

Also:
• Profiles for each child in the house: each one keeps their own scores, Matipoints and achievements.
• Best-score table for each game (only on your phone) and a daily reward.
• Free, no ads and no real-money purchases. Matipoints are earned by playing.
• No chat and no external links. No accounts or personal data, and nothing is sent to any server: your progress is saved on the phone.
• The game is in Spanish.

A game by Matichoc · passion for cacao, from Chile.
